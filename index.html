About me

AI AGENTS · LLMs · AI FOR SCIENCE

I am a direct-PhD student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), School of Artificial Intelligence. My research focuses on AI agents, language model alignment, and AI for science.

I build multi-agent systems for scientific workflows and study how language models can reason with tools and verifiable feedback. My interdisciplinary background in plant protection and economics informs my work on omics intelligence and financial applications.

Previously, I was an algorithm intern at Hithink RoyalFlush, working on financial factor-mining agents and reinforcement learning for language model alignment.
